# Decision Quality Checklist

**Purpose:** Validate quality of decision before finalizing

---

## Process Quality

### Diverse Input
- [ ] Multiple perspectives were genuinely considered
- [ ] Disagreement was surfaced and explored
- [ ] Contrarian view was explicitly examined
- [ ] Devil's Advocate challenged the consensus

### Information Quality
- [ ] Key facts were verified, not assumed
- [ ] Gaps in knowledge were acknowledged
- [ ] Disconfirming evidence was sought
- [ ] Assumptions were made explicit

### Deliberation Quality
- [ ] Sufficient time was taken (not rushed)
- [ ] All relevant advisors contributed
- [ ] Questions preceded advice
- [ ] Experience was shared before solutions

---

## Decision Quality

### Clarity
- [ ] Decision is stated clearly and specifically
- [ ] What we're doing is unambiguous
- [ ] What we're NOT doing is explicit
- [ ] Success criteria are defined

### Rationale
- [ ] Why this decision is documented
- [ ] Key factors that drove the choice are clear
- [ ] Tradeoffs are acknowledged
- [ ] What would change our mind is identified

### Risk Awareness
- [ ] Downside scenarios were considered
- [ ] Worst case is survivable
- [ ] Mitigation strategies exist
- [ ] Margin of safety is adequate

---

## Anti-Bias Checks

### Confirmation Bias
- [ ] We actively looked for disconfirming evidence
- [ ] We didn't just hear what we wanted to hear
- [ ] Advisors with opposing views were included

### Groupthink
- [ ] Dissent was welcomed, not suppressed
- [ ] The room didn't converge too quickly
- [ ] Someone challenged the consensus
- [ ] Alternative options were genuinely explored

### Overconfidence
- [ ] Uncertainty is acknowledged
- [ ] We're not more certain than the evidence warrants
- [ ] "I don't know" was acceptable
- [ ] Prediction ranges, not point estimates

### Availability Bias
- [ ] Recent examples didn't dominate
- [ ] Base rates were considered
- [ ] We looked beyond obvious examples

---

## Action Quality

### Specificity
- [ ] Actions are specific (who, what, by when)
- [ ] First step is clear and immediate
- [ ] Dependencies are identified
- [ ] Resources are allocated

### Accountability
- [ ] Decision owner is named
- [ ] Action owners are assigned
- [ ] Review checkpoint is scheduled
- [ ] Success metrics are defined

### Reversibility
- [ ] We know if this is reversible or not
- [ ] If reversible, exit criteria exist
- [ ] If irreversible, extra scrutiny applied

---

## Munger Filter

Apply Munger's key questions:

- [ ] **Inversão:** What could make this fail? (addressed)
- [ ] **Circle of Competence:** Are we operating within our knowledge?
- [ ] **Second-Order:** What are consequences of consequences?
- [ ] **Opportunity Cost:** What are we NOT doing by choosing this?
- [ ] **Margin of Safety:** If 50% wrong, still acceptable?
