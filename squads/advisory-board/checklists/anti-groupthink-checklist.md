# Anti-Groupthink Checklist

**Purpose:** Prevent consensus-driven decision failures

---

## Warning Signs — During Session

### Too-Fast Agreement
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Unanimous agreement in < 10 minutes
- [ ] **Red Flag:** No one voiced concerns
- [ ] **Red Flag:** "Everyone agrees" was stated early
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Discussion was superficial

### Dominant Voice
- [ ] **Red Flag:** One person drove the conversation
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Junior/minority voices stayed quiet
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Questions to the leader, not debate

### Conformity Pressure
- [ ] **Red Flag:** "Let's not overthink this"
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Eye-rolls at dissent
- [ ] **Red Flag:** "We already know the answer"
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Social pressure to agree

### Echo Chamber
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Only confirming evidence cited
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Similar backgrounds/perspectives
- [ ] **Red Flag:** Outside views dismissed
- [ ] **Red Flag:** "They don't understand our situation"

---

## Mandatory Interventions

### If Warning Signs Detected

| Signal | Intervention | Script |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| Too fast | Slow down | "Before we finalize, let's steelman the opposite position" |
| One voice | Equalize | "I want to hear from [quiet member]" |
| No dissent | Activate DA | "Let's have [X] play devil's advocate" |
| Conformity | Permission | "What's the argument AGAINST what we're leaning towards?" |
| Echo | Outside in | "How would [competitor/critic] view this decision?" |

---

## Devil's Advocate Activation

### When to Activate (Mandatory)
- [ ] Agreement reached without significant debate
- [ ] Stakes are high (hard to reverse, large impact)
- [ ] Decision matches what we "wanted" to decide
- [ ] External pressure to decide quickly
- [ ] Past decision in same area was wrong

### DA Requirements
1. Must genuinely argue opposite position
2. Must provide specific challenges, not token objection
3. Challenges MUST be addressed before proceeding
4. Cannot be overruled by "we don't have time"

## Pre-Mortem Exercise

Before finalizing decision:

```markdown
## PRE-MORTEM

Imagine: It's 12 months from now. This decision has
FAILED SPECTACULARLY.

1. What went wrong?
   - {failure_mode_1}
   - {failure_mode_2}
   - {failure_mode_3}

2. What warning signs did we ignore?
   - {warning_1}
   - {warning_2}

3. What did we assume that turned out wrong?
   - {assumption_1}
   - {assumption_2}

4. Who said "I told you so"?
   - {person/perspective_we_dismissed}
```

## Diversity Check

### Perspective Diversity
- [ ] Aligned AND complementary advisors present
- [ ] At least one natural skeptic included
- [ ] Different expertise areas represented
- [ ] Someone who might be affected negatively included

### Information Diversity
- [ ] Multiple data sources consulted
- [ ] Disconfirming evidence actively sought
- [ ] Outside perspective obtained
- [ ] Historical examples beyond recent memory

---

## Quality of Dissent

### Healthy Dissent Signs
- ✅ Pushback on ideas, not people
- ✅ Specific concerns with evidence
- ✅ Alternative proposals offered
- ✅ Dissenter heard and responded to
- ✅ Dissent improved the final decision

### Unhealthy Dissent Signs
- ❌ Personal attacks or dismissal
- ❌ Vague "I don't like it"
- ❌ Dissent ignored or steamrolled
- ❌ Punished for disagreeing
- ❌ Dissent seen as disloyalty

## Groupthink Recovery

If groupthink detected after decision:

1. **Pause** — Don't implement yet
2. **Reconvene** — With explicit anti-groupthink measures
3. **Add voices** — Bring in someone who wasn't there
4. **Formal DA** — Assign someone to argue against
5. **Revisit** — Only after genuine reconsideration

---

## Reference: Classic Groupthink Symptoms

From Irving Janis's research:

1. **Illusion of invulnerability** — Excessive optimism
2. **Collective rationalization** — Dismissing warnings
3. **Belief in inherent morality** — "We're the good guys"
4. **Stereotyped views of opposition** — "They don't get it"
5. **Pressure on dissenters** — Subtle or explicit
6. **Self-censorship** — Not voicing doubts
7. **Illusion of unanimity** — Silence = agreement
8. **Self-appointed mindguards** — Shielding from contrary info
