# Pre-Meeting Checklist

**Purpose:** Ensure quality session preparation before convening the board

---

## Issue/Topic Preparation

### Clarity Check
- [ ] Issue can be stated in ONE clear sentence
- [ ] Background is factual, not opinion-heavy
- [ ] Desired outcome is specific (decision, direction, validation)

### Context Provided
- [ ] Relevant history/prior decisions included
- [ ] Key constraints identified
- [ ] Timeline/urgency stated

### Prior Work
- [ ] What's been tried is documented
- [ ] Results of prior attempts noted
- [ ] Current thinking articulated

---

## Session Type Selection

### Choose Correct Format
- [ ] **Board Meeting** — Multiple perspectives needed, 30-60min
- [ ] **Issue Processing** — Specific problem, Vistage model, 45min
- [ ] **Strategic Review** — Major direction decision, frameworks, 60min
- [ ] **Opportunity Eval** — Specific opportunity to assess, 30-45min
- [ ] **Quick Consult** — Single perspective needed, 5-10min
- [ ] **Crisis Advisory** — Urgent decision, compressed, 15min

---

## Advisor Selection

### For Automated Selection
- [ ] Topic clearly stated for auto-matching
- [ ] Type specified for advisor matrix

### For Manual Selection
- [ ] Selected advisors are relevant to topic
- [ ] Mix of aligned AND complementary perspectives
- [ ] 3-5 advisors (not too few, not too many)
- [ ] Devil's Advocate assigned or auto-assignable

---

## Decision Readiness

### Decision Owner
- [ ] Clear who makes final decision
- [ ] That person is present/engaged

### Decision Context
- [ ] Reversibility understood (can we undo?)
- [ ] Stakes articulated (what's at risk?)
- [ ] Timeline clear (when must we decide?)

### Post-Decision
- [ ] Ready to commit to actions
- [ ] Accountability checkpoint can be scheduled
- [ ] Success metrics definable

---

## Anti-Groupthink Preparation

### Diverse Perspectives
- [ ] At least one advisor who might disagree
- [ ] Contrarian view explicitly welcome
- [ ] Permission to challenge established

### Psychological Safety
- [ ] No "right answer" predetermined
- [ ] Genuine openness to change position
- [ ] Worst ideas can be voiced safely

---

## Final Check

```
Before starting, confirm:

[Y/N] I can state my issue in one sentence
[Y/N] I know what outcome I want (not just "advice")
[Y/N] I'm genuinely open to perspectives I don't expect
[Y/N] I'm prepared to commit to action at the end
[Y/N] I have identified who makes the final decision
```

---

## Red Flags — Do Not Proceed If:

- ❌ Issue is vague or poorly defined
- ❌ Decision already made (seeking validation only)
- ❌ Wrong advisors for the topic
- ❌ No time for proper session
- ❌ Not ready to act on recommendations
