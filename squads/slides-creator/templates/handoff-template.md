# Slides Creator Handoff Template

## Source Domain

- Strategic: narrative thesis, SCQA, story arc, slide-function map.
- Tactical: render mode, template selection, ingestion, PPTX/export constraints.
- Operational: QA gates, veto conditions, release checklist, blocker routing.

## Required Handoff Fields

- producer
- consumer
- artifact_id
- artifact_path
- lifecycle_state
- blockers
- next_action
